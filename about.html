About

Arne Boedt

Principal IT Consultant • C# Architecture & Enterprise AI

I've spent the last 16 years designing and evolving large-scale systems built on the .NET platform. Before founding my independent practice, I led architecture teams at a global industrial technology company and later at a fast-growing fintech scale-up.

My work sits at the intersection of two powerful forces: the maturity of modern C# and .NET as an enterprise platform, and the rapid emergence of practical AI capabilities that can transform how organizations operate.

I believe the best outcomes happen when architecture decisions and AI strategy are considered together — not as separate workstreams. A well-designed .NET platform becomes the perfect foundation for safe, governed, and high-impact AI adoption.

What I believe

Architecture is a team sport

The best architectures emerge from continuous collaboration between architects, engineers, and business stakeholders — not from ivory towers.

AI must earn its place

I only recommend AI capabilities when they solve real problems with clear ROI. Hype has no place in enterprise architecture.

Simplicity scales

The systems that last are the ones that remain understandable by the next generation of engineers who will maintain them.
